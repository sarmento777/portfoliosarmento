Behind the code

Rafael Sarmento.
On Roblox, Sarmento7.

My Roblox projects cover combat, abilities, interfaces and experiments in environment generation. In the katana project, I went beyond attacks: I wrote the combat services, Iai, the sensei AI and the duel flow.

I like separating responsibilities. The NPC and player share the combat services; the Iai path calculation is also shared by client and server. These choices make the rules easier to follow and adjust.

I’m looking to collaborate on programming mechanics and systems for Roblox, from a prototype through integration with the rest of the game.
